Pre-use inspection: the two-part check

HeyGen presenter video · about 90 seconds · Arabic and English

Subtitles: English

Key points

  • Check the forklift before using it on every shift.
  • Visual check with the engine off: forks, mast chains, leaks, tyres, overhead guard, data plate.
  • Operational check: horn, lights, brakes, parking brake, steering, lift and tilt.
  • Found a defect? Do not use the forklift. Tag it out and report it.

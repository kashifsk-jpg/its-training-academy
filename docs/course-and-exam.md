# Course outline and exam bank

Draft for the Forklift Operator (Counterbalance) course: 10 modules and 30 exam questions. Written from general forklift safety practice (OSHA 29 CFR 1910.178 and ANSI/ITSDF B56.1 principles), not from ITS material. An ITS inspector must check every item against ITS's own course and the standard ITS will be accredited to before it goes live.
